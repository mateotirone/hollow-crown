The collapse and blast rules

This is the exact function running the wall above. A block is safe if it sits on the ground, sits on a safe block, or is held up sideways by a safe block within the bridge strength. Anything left over falls. Blasts are strongest where they hit, fade with distance, punch deeper in the direction they travel, and every block has its own toughness, so craters come out ragged.
