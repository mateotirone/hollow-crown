How the wall stands up

This is the exact function running the wall above, and the one the add-on will use. It works course by course from the ground up, then passes the weight back down.

  • Resting: a block on the ground, or on a block that is itself held, is safe.
  • Shoulder: a block next to a resting block sits on its shoulder. Each course over a hole closes in by one block, so the stone above a breach falls away in a stepped triangle and the rest arches over it, the way real masonry fails.
  • Reach: mortar holds a block or two further out (more with diamond and netherite).
  • Side hold: a block held out by mortar alone can't take much weight. Too much on it and it tears away, so long overhangs break off instead of hanging in the air.
  • Weight: every block passes its own weight plus everything above it down to whatever holds it. If that is more than the block's strength it crushes, and the weight moves on to its neighbours. Undermine a tower and it holds for a while, then the last cracked blocks give way and it comes down.
  • Gates hold up the stone over the gateway while they stand. Blow the gate and that stone has to hold itself.
  • Whatever is left with no support falls as connected chunks that tip toward the open side, then shatter where they land.
