# Stage 4: Walls

Fill between the frame. Leave every opening from the plan empty (doors, windows, arches); they are
finished in stage 5. Gable walls above the wall plate wait for the roof (stage 6), because their shape
follows the roof line.

## Principles

- **Texture without noise.** A wall reads best as one clear colour with a little variation, not a
  confetti of blocks. Lesson from the rebuild: busy textures (Castle Stone, bricks) read best almost
  pure, with a different block only at the corners. Large green patches (green wool, moss blocks) read as grass; moss only as a light touch in the bottom courses.
- **Variation follows logic.** Real walls weather at the bottom (damp, moss), around openings (wear) and
  at the corners (dressed stone). Put the variation there, not evenly everywhere.
- **Depth is built in.** Every face longer than 5 has three depth layers. Plan them now (what is set
  back, what is the face, what is proud) even if the proud parts are added in stages 5 and 7.

## Step 1: Infill

- Every cell between posts and beams is the palette's wall main block (or the wall mix).
- Timber regions: infill sits on the wall line, flush with the frame or with the frame proud of it (see
  `02-frame`). Never set infill back from the wall line: it renders as holes.
- Stone regions: the wall is the face; frame stage gave it quoins and string courses.

## Step 2: Texture mixes

Use a weighted mix only where the material really varies. Defaults:

| Wall | Main | Variation (total) | Where the variation goes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plaster infill (timber) | calcite / white concrete / white terracotta (by colour) | 0 to 10% | none needed; plaster is plain. A slightly off-white second block near the ground at most |
| Brick infill | bricks | 10 to 15% mud bricks or granite (warm, same colour) | random, no clusters |
| Rough stone walls | stone bricks or cobble | 20 to 35% (cracked, mossy, andesite, tuff) | mossy and cobble in the bottom 2 courses (more near the ground), cracked around openings |
| Dressed stone (civic, walls) | one smooth block | 0 to 5% | corners only (quoins), string courses |
| Planks (Frostmarch) | spruce planks | 30% stripped spruce log (axis along the wall) | in whole rows, not single blocks |
| Mud brick (Blackfen) | mud bricks | 15 to 30% packed mud | lower half |
| Sandstone (Emberwaste) | smooth sandstone | 30 to 40% sandstone and cut sandstone | random, plus cut sandstone at corners and openings |

Rules:

- Never more than 3 blocks in one wall mix.
- The main block is at least 60% of any wall.
- Mixes must be colour-close: the eye should see one material. Pick by colour (Mateo: any solid block of
  the right colour); test it in the render.

## Step 3: Depth layers

Pick at least three for every long face. Typical stacks:

- **Timber house**: plinth (proud) / infill (face) / frame (face or proud) / jetty (proud) /
  sills and eaves (proud).
- **Stone house**: plinth or batter (proud) / wall and glass (face) / window surrounds, string course and
  sills (proud) / buttresses (proud).
- **Civic**: base storey (proud) / pilasters or piers between bays (proud by 1) / wall between (face) /
  framed windows (surrounds proud) / cornice under the eaves (proud).

## Step 4: Gables (below the roof line only)

- The gable wall above the wall plate is filled in stage 6, once the roof line exists. Here, make sure
  the wall plate beam is at the right height and the gable posts (centre post on the ridge line, plus the
  bay posts carried up) are marked in the plan.
- Gable infill can differ from the walls: vertical planks or stripped logs (Frostmarch), weatherboard
  (spruce or dark oak slabs/trapdoors) or the same plaster. Decide in the plan.

## Step 5: Chimney breasts and wall stacks

- If the plan has a chimney on an outside wall, build its breast now: 3 wide at the bottom (odd), stepping
  to 1 wide above the fireplace with a stair on each shoulder, full height to the roof. Stone from the
  palette's chimney mix, rougher than the walls.

## Region notes

- **Vale**: plaster style = calcite infill only (diorite reads as grey patches); stone style = stone
  bricks with cobble and moss low; brick style = bricks between stripped spruce posts.
- **Frostmarch**: plank infill in rows; stone in dark cobbled deepslate and polished deepslate.
- **Saltreach**: stone ground storey in pale stone; upper walls whitewash between birch posts.
- **Blackfen**: mud brick and packed mud; mangrove plank gables.
- **Emberwaste**: thick sandstone walls, slightly darker band at the base, no frame.
- **Hollow**: veinstone bricks with cracked veinstone and blackstone; pale ash between dark oak.

## Before you move on

- [ ] Every wall closed except the openings in the plan.
- [ ] Mixes: max 3 blocks, main at least 60%, variation where it belongs (base, openings, corners).
- [ ] Three depth layers planned or built on every face over 5 long.
- [ ] Gable posts marked for stage 6; chimney breasts built.
- [ ] Look at each face straight on: one clear material, no blotches, no large green patches.
