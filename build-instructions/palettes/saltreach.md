# Saltreach (Gull's Rest)

Harbour coast. Mediterranean and Atlantic harbour look: wind-proof pale stone ground storeys,
whitewashed upper walls between birch frames, low hipped sea-green roofs, white and orange roofs among
them, domes and a lighthouse on civic buildings, painted shutters.

## Shapes

Hipped roofs pitch 1, flat roof terraces on harbour houses, arcades of 3-wide arches on civic fronts,
domes from hall level 2, a lighthouse tower from level 3.
