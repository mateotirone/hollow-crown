# Blackfen (Mirelight)

Black water marsh. Stilt houses of mangrove and mud brick lifted above the wet ground, mossy low roofs,
few small windows, the green glow of froglights that give Mirelight its name. Dark, damp, a little
uneasy at night.

## Roof colours available

| Colour | Stairs / slab / block |
|---|---|
| Mossy dark shingle | `dark_oak_stairs` with `moss_block` on the ridge and 10 to 15% moss on the slope (`settle_halls.thatch`) |
| Mangrove | `mangrove_stairs` / `mangrove_slab` / `mangrove_planks` |
| Mud tile | `mud_brick_stairs` / `mud_brick_slab` / `mud_bricks` |
| Thatch (accent) | `hay_block` with `dark_oak_stairs` edges on barns and stores |

|---|---|

| Halls | `green_stained_glass_pane` |
| Stores | trapdoor lattice, no glass |

## Shapes

Gables pitch 1 with low eaves, stilts under every corner and bay, stairs up to a 3 x 1 landing, boardwalks
between houses.
