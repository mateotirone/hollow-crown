# Emberwaste (Sunscar)

Hot red desert, the dragon region. North African kasbah look: thick sandstone walls, flat roofs with
parapets and roof terraces, tapering towers, small deep windows behind lattice, projecting roof beams,
striped awnings, domes on civic buildings.

## Roof colours available

| Colour | Stairs / slab / block |
|---|---|
| Flat sand roof | `smooth_sandstone` with a `smooth_sandstone_slab` parapet and `sandstone_wall` corners |
| Red dome / pitched | `red_sandstone_stairs`, `smooth_red_sandstone_stairs` / matching slabs / `cut_red_sandstone` |
| Blue-green dome (civic accent) | `cyan_terracotta` body, `waxed_oxidized_cut_copper_stairs` near the top |
| Gold finial | `gold_block` single block on a dome top (civic only) |

## Shapes

Flat roofs with crenellated parapets, roof hatches and ladders, tapering kasbah towers with corner horns,
domes, arched doorways, courtyards.
