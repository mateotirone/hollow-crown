Tree of Oaths screen

A working mock of the skill tree screen as it would open at the Tree of Oaths. Pick a branch, click an oath, swear it. The player shown is an example: a Marked at Renown 14 with a Keep and 7 points to spend. Colours and frames are placeholders until the menu art is chosen; the layout and behaviour are what to react to.

Square: always onRound: a power you castColoured frame: swornFilled: masteredBright frame: you can swear it nowDim: locked

How it works

One screen, four branches.Keep and Wayfarer are open to everyone. Blade belongs to Knights and Arcana to the Marked; the other one shows as closed, so you can see what you gave up.
Rings read top to bottom.Each ring names what opens it (hall and Renown). A closed ring is dimmed and says what is missing in red.
Lines show order.A line lights up once the oath above it is sworn. Pips under a node are its ranks.
The right panel is the decision.What it does at each rank, the cost, the origin line for Arcana powers, and one button. If it is locked, the reason replaces the button.
