The Sorcerer's Power Tree

Every power on the magic path, the order it is gained and what opens it. A player enters this path by taking the Ember into their hand after their first siege. Powers are bought at the Tree of Oaths with skill points; each ring opens when the town hall and the player's Renown are both high enough. Learning all of it costs 82 points.

Power you cast (raises Resonance)Passive, always onThe peak: lets you rule the Hollow

Ranks along the way

The title changes with points spent in this tree. Only a Sorcerer can wear the Hollow Crown.

  1. Touched, from the start
  2. Listener, 3 points
  3. Adept, 8 points
  4. Veilwalker, 15 points
  5. Sorcerer, 26 points
