What is the game's, and what is ours
The add-on's real scripts run here unchanged, so who comes, when and where, the war plan, wall damage and collapse, rams, sappers, ballistas, guard posts, the Keep's health, win and loss are the game's. Creatures take their health, speed, attacks and targets from the pack's own entity files, and are drawn from the resource pack's own models, textures and animations. Ours, written to Minecraft's rules: walking and path-finding, hit timing, arrow flight. Not drawn: items in hands, the game's particles (shown as puffs) and sounds.
