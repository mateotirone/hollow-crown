The seven bosses

Each has a boss bar, phases, attacks that warn you before they land, and something you have to work out. Everyone who fought gets the named relic the first time they win, plus 5 skill points; a vault chest with the shared hoard appears where the boss fell. Walk away for 30 seconds and the fight resets.

Aldmere Vale · 160 hearts

The Clockwork Reeve

Millbrook's town clock, given legs. It is keeping one minute safe, and it will not let that minute end.

  • Time-stop: the clock strikes and lit tiles appear. Anyone not standing on one is frozen for 4 seconds and hurt.
  • At 60% health his gears fly apart into three Cogs. He can't be hurt until all three are scrap.
  • At 30% he's overwound: faster, and the clock strikes more often.

Relic Minute Hand: a sword whose hits slow time around the target.

Starts with: Stopped Clock Key

Frostmarch · 180 hearts

The Pale Huntsman

He led Rimehold's hunters onto the frozen lake one night, and only he walked back off it.

  • He vanishes into the blizzard for 7 seconds at a time. Only his breath shows.
  • Marked shot: he picks a player; get a wall between you in 2.5 seconds or take 6 hearts.
  • At 60% Rimewolves join. At 25% he plants three Rime Pillars and is untouchable until you break them all, then he's stunned for 8 seconds.

Relic Rimefang Bow: arrows root targets in ice.

Starts with: Pale Horn

Saltreach · 160 hearts

The Drowned Bride

The lighthouse keeper still writes to her. She has been answering, from three hundred years under the water.

  • A Tide meter climbs all fight, faster each phase: slowed at 40%, crippled at 70%, drowning at 100%.
  • Strike all three Chapel Bells within 10 seconds of each other to turn the tide back to zero.
  • She wails to drag you in, hands grab from the water, drowned climb out. At high tide in the last phase she heals.

Relic Widow's Veil: breathe water, see in the dark, and see Hollow things marked (it also reveals the real Mother).

Starts with: Drowned Wedding Ring

Emberwaste · 210 hearts

The Ash Seneschal

Sunscar chiseled a face off every statue. The Seneschal chiseled off his own.

  • Every 15 seconds he studies what hurt him most. Blades: he shrugs off 65% of melee and answers with cleaves. Arrows: 85% of arrow damage gone and he rains volleys.
  • So you have to switch weapons mid-fight, or split the party.
  • At 50% patches of sand give way: get out before they drop.

Relic Seneschal's Greatshield (sneak with it in your offhand to brace and throw attackers back) and a Faceless Crown Shard.

Starts with: Faceless Seal

Blackfen · 190 hearts

The Mother of Changelings

Every changeling in Mirelight is one of her children. She hides among them, and she has a true name she would kill to keep.

  • She hides among 5 to 8 copies of herself. Hit the real one to stun her. Hit a copy and it bursts into poison and a Changeling.
  • Take over 25 seconds and she heals and poisons everyone.
  • The real one sheds a faint spore. Echo Sight, her true name or the Widow's Veil lights her up for that player only.

Relic Mire Heart: an offhand charm that heals you with every hit at night.

Starts with: Mire Effigy

Grand Siege · 150 hearts

The Warlord King

Something in his crown catches the light wrong. He has lost before. He keeps coming back.

  • Rides in on a Siege Ogre, taking far less damage while it lives.
  • At 66% he fights on foot: shard lances to sidestep, and the crown tolls up more Footmen and a Bell-Ringer.
  • At 30%: Crown Quake. Be in the air when it lands.

Relic The Warlord's Brand: sets foes alight, and every fifth hit is a shockwave.

Starts with: the Grand Siege

The Hollow · 500 hearts

Morvane

He holds the Seat at the bottom of the Hollow, and he has been waiting a long time for someone to get this far.

  • A duel. When he raises his guard, don't swing: it comes straight back at you and heals him.
  • At 66% and 33% the hall lurches and something steps out of him. You're thrown across the hall and must beat it before he can be hurt again.
  • Last phase: a toll goes through the hall. Only the quiet tiles are safe.

Relic Knights get the Oath of Oswin (executes weak foes, rallies guards). The Marked get the Crownless Mantle (one hit in five passes through you).

Starts with: the Drowned Court, deep in the Hollow

The eighth boss, the Choir, is being built with the Hollow (the world below) on this same fight framework.

NPC fighters

Stand-ins for other players when you play alone or a seat is empty: rival lords, their armies, an NPC ruler's champions. They come in five tiers and get tougher per extra player nearby. They can be allies, neutral or enemies. They can't place or break blocks.

Rival Knight

40 hearts · 3.5 hearts a hit

Fights like you do: sword up close, bow at range, a shield in between, and a potion when it goes badly. Bring an axe for the shield.

Sword up close, bow at range. Raises a shield in cycles (an axe breaks it for 5 seconds). Strafes, hops back after a hit, drinks a potion when low (hit them mid-drink to spoil it), pearls after you if you run. War-cry and shield bash.

Rival Sorcerer

32 hearts · 2.5 hearts a hit

Keeps its distance, throws Veilbolts, marks the ground under your feet and steps through the air when you close in.

Keeps its distance and throws Veilbolts that lead your movement. Marks the ground under you: two seconds to move. Blinks away when cornered. Drinks potions too.

Rival Lord

55 hearts · 4 hearts a hit

A lord with a keep of their own. Everything a Rival Knight does, plus retainers who come when called.

Everything the Rival Knight does with more health, and calls two Retainers at 65% and again at 30%.

Retainer

18 hearts · 2.5 hearts a hit

A lord's man-at-arms. Uses a shield properly, which is more than most things out here.

A lord's man-at-arms: shield cycles and strafing, no bow and no potions. The rank and file of an NPC army.

Choir-called raiders

Take over your sieges: 15% of the army from your 3rd siege, 15% more each siege after, up to 85%.

Tollman

13 hearts · 2.5 hearts a hit

The first to answer the bell. They march with their eyes open and nobody home behind them.

The rank and file. Nothing special, lots of them.

Bell-Ringer

10 hearts · 1 hearts a hit

Every ring of its bell makes the raiders around it braver and faster. Kill it first. It knows this, and runs.

Every 3 seconds its bell gives raiders within 10 blocks Speed and Strength. Runs from you.

Shieldwall Brute

27 hearts · 4.5 hearts a hit

A tower shield and nothing else to think about. Arrows bounce. Get behind him.

Takes a quarter damage from arrows. Slow, hits like a cart.

Torchbearer

11 hearts · 2 hearts a hit

Sets every wooden thing it passes alight. Stone walls were built for a reason.

Lights wooden blocks it walks past and sets you on fire. Immune to fire.

Siege Ogre

60 hearts · 7 hearts a hit

Carries a whole tree trunk as a ram. Walls crack where it walks.

Replaces the ravager as the battering ram: its walk cracks walls. 120 health.

Crow-Mage

12 hearts · 1.5 hearts a hit

Calls a murder of Bellcrows and throws blinding bolts from the back of the line.

Throws blinding bolts from range and summons Bellcrows (they fade after 30 seconds).

Grey Warlord

30 hearts · 4.5 hearts a hit

A named war-chief who heard the bell louder than the rest.

Named warlords now use this body instead of a vindicator.

Things from the Hollow

Walk the night after the first boss falls anywhere, and haunt the Hollow (the world below). This is where the unease lives.

Lost Villager

10 hearts · 3 hearts a hit

One of the Lost Days. It just stands there facing you. It is never where you left it when you look back.

Frozen while you look at it. Look away and it steps closer. If it reaches you unseen it strikes and the world goes dark.

Changeling

14 hearts · 3 hearts a hit

Something wearing a villager. It gets the face nearly right. Nearly.

Looks like a villager until you turn your back within 5 blocks, or hit it. Then it shows its real face.

Wrongling

20 hearts · 5 hearts a hit

It only moves when nobody is looking. Don't blink. Don't turn around. Don't go alone.

Only moves when nobody is looking. While watched it's frozen and takes a quarter damage. Fast and brutal the moment you blink.

Stone Vigil

30 hearts · 4 hearts a hit

The Order carved them to keep watch over its dead. Something else is keeping watch through them now. It is only a statue while somebody is looking.

A winged stone mourner. While anyone is looking at it, it's a statue and only a pickaxe can chip it. Look away, or get blinded, and it crosses the room. Its touch throws you somewhere else nearby, in the dark, away from your friends.

Echo Hound

12 hearts · 2.5 hearts a hit

Blind. It hunts by the hum of the Ember. The more of the Ember you carry, the farther away it hears you.

Blind. Tracks Marked players with loud Resonance from 64 blocks away, in pairs.

Mirror Knight

22 hearts · 3.5 hearts a hit

Whatever you hit it with last, it learns. Shoot it and it shoots back. Strike it and it parries. Change it up.

Learns your last attack type: sword it and swords stop working, shoot it and arrows do. Alternate.

Tolling Shade

15 hearts · 2 hearts a hit

You have heard that bell before, under your own keep. Each ring drags at your legs and darkens the world.

Floats. Every 5 seconds its bell slows you and darkens your screen.

Gloamwing

7 hearts · 1.5 hearts a hit

They ride the fog over the Hollow and leave new arrivals alone. Stay too long and they stop.

A ray-shaped flyer that lives in the Hollow's fog. Stay in the Hollow five minutes and the first one finds you; another joins every two minutes, up to six. They swoop, hit lightly and wheel away. Leave and they give up. Each drops a Gloamquill.

Seep

30 hearts

A place where the Hollow is pushing through. Things come out of it. Left alone, it widens.

Not a mob: a crack where the Hollow pushes through. One opens somewhere near a player every so often, day or night, alone or with friends. Things from the Hollow climb out of it. Kill its guards, then break it to close it. Ignore it and it widens, then splits.

Regional beasts

Spawn naturally in their region's biomes.

Barrow Wight

17 hearts · 3 hearts a hit · Aldmere Vale

A knight buried with his crown and his grudge. Every hit it lands heals it. The Vale's barrows are full of them.

Heals with every hit it lands. Burns in daylight.

Frost Stag

15 hearts · 3.5 hearts a hit · Frostmarch

Gentle until you strike it. Its Pale Antlers are worth a fortune to any Stables master.

Peaceful until hit. Drops Pale Antler for horse gear.

Rimewolf

11 hearts · 2 hearts a hit · Frostmarch

Hunts in packs through the snow. Their bite freezes your legs, and they know it.

Pack hunter. Bites slow you.

Brine Lurker

17 hearts · 3 hearts a hit · Saltreach

Lies still in the shallows like a rock. When you walk past, it drags you under.

Amphibious ambusher. Bites slow you and drag you down in water.

Ash Scarab

3 hearts · 1 hearts a hit · Emberwaste

One is nothing. They are never one. Their bites smoulder, and their shells are half glass.

Swarms of 4 to 7. Bites set you alight.

Dune Wyrm

45 hearts · 5 hearts a hit · Emberwaste

The sand ripples. Then the sand stands up. It hunts by the shake of your footsteps, so stand still or run.

Feels footsteps: keep moving and it erupts under you. Stand still or sneak and it loses you. 90 health.

Bog Hag

15 hearts · 1.5 hearts a hit · Blackfen

Throws poison and lays hexes. Old Mag says the hags were her sisters once. Old Mag says a lot of things.

Throws poison and lays hexes (weakness, hunger, nausea).

Mire Toad

8 hearts · 1.5 hearts a hit · Blackfen

Its tongue reaches six blocks. Its skin is poison, so punching it is a mistake.

Tongue yanks you in from 6 blocks. Punching it poisons you.

Order of the Ashen Gate

Friend to Knights, enemy to the Marked. Placed at outposts by the map, not spawned.

Ashen Squire

12 hearts · 2.5 hearts a hit

Young, loyal, and certain the Order has nothing to hide.

Fights monsters. Attacks only Marked players or anyone who attacks the Order.

Ashen Knight

22 hearts · 4 hearts a hit

Sworn to the Ashen Gate. An honest blade. Ask what the Gate guards and the conversation ends.

Same loyalties as the Squire, with plate that halves arrow damage.

Inquisitor

18 hearts · 2 hearts a hit

Hunts Sorcerers. Near one, the Ember goes quiet: no spells, nothing answering, just you and a crossbow.

Crossbow. Marked players within 10 blocks are silenced (no spells).

Try them

  1. In a flat creative world, type /scriptevent siege:showcase. Every mob and boss stands behind you as a labelled statue that can't move or fight, so you can walk around and look.
  2. To fight one for real, use its spawn egg.
  3. Place a boss egg on open flat ground and the fight starts where it stands. Or hold a boss key and use it.
  4. Other features can start a fight with /scriptevent siege:bestiary_summon reeve (also huntsman, bride, seneschal, mother, warlord_king, oswin).

What is still placeholder

  • Every skin is a flat-colour placeholder on a box model. All of them are on the texture list for the one big art pass.
  • Nothing here has been run inside Minecraft yet. It passes the offline checks, and the first in-game session will turn up things to fix.
  • Fights use marked circles and props instead of changing blocks, so they work anywhere. When the map has real arenas (a chapel that floods, a frozen lake) they can use them.
