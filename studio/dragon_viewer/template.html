Down, not dead

  • Unconscious: sprawled on its side, wings limp, chest rising fast, bleeding. A golden apple from its owner or an ally stops the bleeding and it wakes a minute later.
  • Left alone it bleeds out in about ten minutes. An enemy can finish it where it lies.
  • Dead: the same sprawl rolled further, jaw slack, nothing moving. The body stays until the fighting is over, then crumbles to ash.
