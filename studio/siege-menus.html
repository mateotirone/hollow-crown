Siege Nights menus

What the new menus look like in Minecraft, filled in with example numbers. In the game they use Bedrock's own window style, so the exact look can differ slightly by device. Buttons here do nothing.

Keep menu. Click your Keep Banner with anything except dye.

Barracks menu. Click a Barracks with an empty hand. Clicking it holding emeralds still recruits straight away.

Barracks

Guards: 6/8 Your emeralds: 23 Recruiting costs 5 emeralds and needs an unemployed villager standing next to the Barracks. Men-at-Arms wait here and charge out during sieges. Archers stay wherever you place them.
Your guards. From either menu. Lists every guard near you with their armor and where they stand.

Your guards
