How players get it

Any region's Smith reforges a piece in that region's style for a few Regals, and keeps its enchantments, wear and name. Or at the Workshop: the piece plus that region's Castle Stone. Upgrading diamond to netherite, or netherite to Choirsteel, at a smithing table keeps the region.
