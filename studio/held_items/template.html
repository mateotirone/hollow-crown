What changed

Held items used to hang on the forearm, and Bedrock then drew them floating at the chest or face. Now they hang on the upper arm. A hand that holds something keeps its elbow straight in the custom moves (the bend goes to the shoulder). An empty hand still bends. Shields go in the off-hand bone, so the Seneschal's Greatshield and the vanilla shield are covered by the same fix.

Where exactly an item sits inside the hand is drawn close to vanilla, but the game decides the last few pixels. Your own skin uses the same bone layout, so it holds things the same way.
