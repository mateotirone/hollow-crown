The Hollow Crown

Who comes to Kingsrest?

Everyone arrives the same way: on the coach, with one bag, as nobody in particular. What you become after that is up to you and the land. Answer a few questions and this will suggest who you were before you got here.

  • A name, and what people call you behind your back
  • What you look like, as a painted portrait and as a skin you can wear in the game
  • The road you are likely to walk: Knight, Sorcerer, Dragon Rider, Forgemaster, Captain or Witch
  • Which of the twelve settlement plots to claim, and which train gets you there

About four minutes. Nothing here is binding. You can change every part of the result.
